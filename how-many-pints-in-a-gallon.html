    How many pints in a gallon?

    1 US liquid gallon = 8 US pints. Same gallon: 4 quarts, 16 cups, 128 fluid ounces, 3.785 L. Half a gallon is 4 pints. Fluid ounces here are volume, not weight ounces.

    Gotchas

    • US pint vs imperial pint. US liquid pint = 16 US fl oz ≈ 473.176 mL. Imperial (UK) pint = 20 imperial fl oz ≈ 568.261 mL. This page is US liquid.
    • US gallon vs imperial gallon. US gal = 3.785411784 L = 8 US pints. Imperial gal = 4.54609 L = 8 imperial pints (about 9.6 US pints). Do not mix the two eights.
    • Fluid ounces ≠ weight ounces. 8 pints is 128 fl oz of volume. A gallon of milk and a gallon of oil do not weigh the same.

    FAQ

    How many pints are in a gallon?

    There are 8 US liquid pints in 1 US liquid gallon (3.785411784 L). That is 4 quarts or 16 cups.

    Is a US pint the same as an imperial pint?

    No. A US liquid pint is 16 US fl oz (473.176 mL). An imperial (UK) pint is 20 imperial fl oz (568.261 mL). An imperial gallon is 8 imperial pints (4.54609 L), not 8 US pints.

    How many pints are in a half gallon?

    4 US pints. A half gallon is also 2 quarts, 8 cups, or 64 US fluid ounces.
