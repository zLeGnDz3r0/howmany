    How many countries are there?

    There are 193 UN member states (United Nations Member States list; last admission South Sudan, 14 July 2011). That is a membership count. “How many countries exist?” depends on whether you add observers, de facto states, or disputed territories. This page does not invent a second number.

    Gotchas

    • 193 is UN membership, not a geography quiz. Taiwan, Kosovo, Western Sahara, and similar cases are not UN member states. Adding them is a political choice, not a hidden UN figure.
    • Observers are not members. The Holy See and the State of Palestine have standing invitations as non-member observer states. 193 + 2 = 195 is a sum people make; the UN still lists 193 members.
    • Sports and phone-code lists differ. FIFA, ISO 3166, and the Olympics each have their own membership. Those are not 193 unless they happen to match. Cite the list you used.

    FAQ

    How many countries are there?

    The United Nations has 193 member states (UN Member States list; last admission South Sudan, 14 July 2011). That is a membership count. Other answers depend on how you count observers, de facto states, and disputed territories.

    When did the UN reach 193 members?

    2011, when South Sudan was admitted on 14 July 2011 (UN growth in membership; UN Member States list). The public list still shows 193 members as of 1 September 2026.

    What about Vatican City and Palestine?

    They are not UN member states. The UN lists the Holy See and the State of Palestine as non-member observer states with standing invitations to the General Assembly.

    Why do some lists say 195 or 197?

    Those lists usually add observers or entities that are not UN members. This page does not invent a second total. If you need a number, cite what you counted (UN members, members + observers, FIFA, ISO, …).
