    How many pints in a quart?

    1 US liquid quart = 2 US pints. Same quart: 4 cups, 32 fluid ounces, 946.353 mL. Half a quart is 1 pint. Fluid ounces here are volume, not weight ounces.

    Gotchas

    • US pint vs imperial pint. US liquid pint = 16 US fl oz ≈ 473.176 mL. Imperial (UK) pint = 20 imperial fl oz ≈ 568.261 mL. This page is US liquid.
    • US quart vs imperial quart. US liquid quart = 2 US pints = 32 US fl oz ≈ 946.353 mL. Imperial quart = 2 imperial pints ≈ 1.1365225 L. Do not mix the two “2 pints” answers across systems.
    • Fluid ounces ≠ weight ounces. 2 pints is 32 fl oz of volume. A quart of milk and a quart of oil do not weigh the same.

    FAQ

    How many pints are in a quart?

    There are 2 US liquid pints in 1 US liquid quart (946.353 mL). That is 4 cups or 32 fluid ounces.

    Is a US pint the same as an imperial pint?

    No. A US liquid pint is 16 US fl oz (473.176 mL). An imperial (UK) pint is 20 imperial fl oz (568.261 mL). An imperial quart is 2 imperial pints (about 1.137 L), not 2 US pints.

    How many cups are in a quart?

    4 US cups. A quart is also 2 pints or 32 US fluid ounces.
