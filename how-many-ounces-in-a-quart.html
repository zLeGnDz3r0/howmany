    How many ounces in a quart?

    1 US liquid quart = 32 US fluid ounces. Same quart: 2 pints, 4 cups, 64 tablespoons, 946.353 mL. Half a quart is 16 fl oz (1 pint). Fluid ounces here are volume, not weight ounces.

    Gotchas

    • US quart vs imperial quart. US liquid quart = 32 US fl oz ≈ 946.353 mL (0.946352946 L). Imperial (UK) quart = 40 imperial fl oz ≈ 1.1365225 L. This page is US liquid.
    • Liquid quart vs dry quart. A US dry quart is a different unit (~1.101 L) used for some produce. Kitchen “quart” for milk, stock, and recipes almost always means the liquid quart (32 fl oz / 4 cups).
    • Fluid ounces ≠ weight ounces. 32 fl oz is the volume of a US quart. That is not 32 ounces on a scale unless the ingredient’s density makes it so.

    FAQ

    How many ounces are in a quart?

    There are 32 US fluid ounces in 1 US liquid quart (946.353 mL). That is volume, not 32 ounces of weight.

    Is a US quart the same as an imperial quart?

    No. A US liquid quart is 32 US fl oz (946.353 mL). An imperial (UK) quart is 40 imperial fl oz (about 1.1365225 L).

    Are fluid ounces the same as weight ounces?

    No. 32 fl oz is the volume of a US quart. That is not 32 ounces on a scale unless the ingredient’s density makes it so.
