    How many square feet in an acre?

    1 acre = 43,560 square feet (NIST Handbook 44, exactly). Same acre: 160 square rods, 10 square chains. It is an amount of area, not a required shape.

    Fractions work: 1/2, 1/4. Result is square feet.

    Gotchas

    • Not a square. NIST: an acre is 43,560 sq ft of area. If it happens to be square, a side is about 208.710 ft (they note that is not exact).
    • 640 acres in a square mile. 1 mi² = 27,878,400 sq ft = 640 acres (NIST).
    • International vs survey foot. 43,560 sq ft is the definition. In metres, NIST lists 4,046.8564224 m² (international foot) vs a slightly larger survey-foot acre. US survey foot was deprecated for most new work; old plats may still use it.

    Sources

    FAQ

    How many square feet are in an acre?

    43,560 square feet in 1 acre (NIST Handbook 44).

    Is an acre a square?

    No. It is an area. If square, each side is about 208.710 ft (NIST; not exact).

    How many acres in a square mile?

    640 acres = 1 square mile (NIST).

    How many square metres in an acre?

    4,046.8564224 m² using the international foot (NIST).
