    How many cups in a gallon?

    1 US gallon = 16 US cups. Same gallon: 8 pints, 4 quarts, 128 fluid ounces, 3.785 L. Fluid ounces are volume, not weight ounces.

    Gotchas

    • US cup vs 250 mL metric cup. 16 US cups = 1 US gallon (3.785 L). Sixteen 250 mL cups = 4.0 L — more than a US gallon. Recipes that use metric cups will overshoot if you treat them as US cups.
    • US gallon vs imperial gallon. US gal = 3.785411784 L. Imperial gal = 4.54609 L (about 19.2 US cups).
    • Fluid ounces ≠ weight ounces. 16 cups is 128 fl oz of volume. A gallon of milk and a gallon of oil do not weigh the same.
