    How many teaspoons in a gallon?

    1 US liquid gallon = 768 US teaspoons. Same gallon: 16 cups, 8 pints, 4 quarts, 256 tablespoons, 128 fluid ounces, 3,785.412 mL. Half a gallon is 384 tsp. Fluid ounces here are volume, not weight ounces.

    Gotchas

    • US gallon ≠ imperial gallon. US liquid gallon = 128 US fl oz ≈ 3,785.412 mL = 768 US tsp. Imperial (UK) gallon = 160 imperial fl oz ≈ 4.546 L. This page is US liquid.
    • Teaspoon size. US customary teaspoon ≈ 4.929 mL. Many metric recipes use a 5 mL teaspoon — close, but not identical if you scale a big batch.
    • Fluid ounces ≠ weight ounces. 768 tsp is 128 fl oz of volume. A gallon of water and a gallon of honey do not weigh the same.

    FAQ

    How many teaspoons are in a gallon?

    There are 768 US teaspoons in 1 US liquid gallon (3,785.412 mL). That is 16 cups, 8 pints, 4 quarts, 256 tablespoons, or 128 fluid ounces.

    How many teaspoons are in half a gallon?

    384 teaspoons. A quarter gallon (1 quart) is 192 teaspoons.

    Is a US gallon the same as an imperial gallon?

    No. A US liquid gallon is 128 US fl oz (3,785.412 mL), so 768 US teaspoons. An imperial (UK) gallon is 160 imperial fl oz (about 4.546 L) — a different size.
