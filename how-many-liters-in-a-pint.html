    How many liters in a pint?

    1 US liquid pint = 0.473176 L. Same pint: 473.176 mL, 16 fluid ounces, 2 cups, 32 tablespoons, 96 teaspoons. That is exactly 3.785411784 / 8 L (the US liquid gallon of 231 in³), shown to the same rounding as 473.176 mL. This page is the US liquid pint — not the imperial (UK) pint of about 0.568261 L, and not the US dry pint.

    Gotchas

    • US liquid pint vs imperial pint. US liquid pint = 16 US fl oz = 0.473176 L. Imperial (UK) pint = 20 imperial fl oz ≈ 0.568261 L. This page is US liquid only.
    • Liters are volume. 0.473176 L of water is about 473.2 g near 4 °C, but a pint of flour or honey is a different weight.
    • Liquid pint vs dry pint. A US dry pint is a different measure (~0.550610 L) used for some produce. This page is the US liquid pint (16 fl oz / 2 cups).

    FAQ

    How many liters are in a pint?

    1 US liquid pint = 0.473176 L (exactly 3.785411784 / 8 L). That is 473.176 mL, 16 US fluid ounces, 2 US cups, 32 tablespoons, or 96 teaspoons.

    Is a US pint the same as an imperial pint?

    No. A US liquid pint is 16 US fl oz (0.473176 L). An imperial (UK) pint is 20 imperial fl oz (about 0.568261 L). This page is the US liquid pint.

    How many liters are in half a pint?

    0.236588 L (236.588 mL, 8 US fl oz, 1 US cup). A quarter pint is 0.118294 L.
