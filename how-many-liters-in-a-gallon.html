    How many liters in a gallon?

    1 US liquid gallon = 3.785411784 liters (NIST Handbook 44: the gallon is 231 cubic inches). 1 imperial gallon = 4.54609 L (UK). They are not interchangeable. Default here is US; switch the gallon standard for imperial.

    Using US liquid gallon: 1 gal = 3.785411784 L = 231 in³ (NIST). Do not mix with the imperial gallon.

    Fractions work: 1/2, 1/4. Result is liters for the selected gallon.

    Gotchas

    • US and imperial gallons are different. US: 3.785411784 L (231 in³). Imperial: 4.54609 L. A UK gallon is about 20% larger. Do not mix fuel, paint, or recipe figures across the two.
    • Liquid, not dry. NIST’s 231 in³ gallon is the US liquid gallon. The US dry gallon is a different unit (268.8025 in³ in NIST tables).
    • Cups still follow the cup standard. This page converts gallons to liters. Kitchen cups on this site default to US customary (236.588 mL), not a 250 mL metric cup. See the converter.

    FAQ

    How many liters are in a gallon?

    1 US liquid gallon = 3.785411784 L (NIST). 1 imperial gallon = 4.54609 L (UK). They are not the same.

    Is that a US gallon or an imperial gallon?

    This page defaults to the US liquid gallon. Switch the gallon standard for the imperial (UK) gallon. Do not mix them.

    How many milliliters are in a US gallon?

    3,785.411784 mL. Same figure as 3.785411784 L. 1 L = 1,000 mL.

    Is the US dry gallon the same?

    No. This page is liquid gallons. The US dry gallon is a different unit (268.8025 in³ in NIST tables), not 231 in³.
