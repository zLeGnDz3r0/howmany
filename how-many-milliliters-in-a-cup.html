    How many milliliters in a cup?

    1 US customary cup = 236.588 mL. Same cup: 8 fluid ounces, 16 tablespoons, 48 teaspoons. That is the exact US liquid cup (1/16 of a US gallon). A metric cup is 250 mL; the US nutrition-label cup is 240 mL.

    Gotchas

    • US cup vs 240 mL vs 250 mL. US customary cup = 236.588 mL (8 US fl oz). US legal cup on nutrition labels = 240 mL. Metric cup = 250 mL. NIST home-cooking charts often round the US cup to 240 mL — close, but not the exact customary value used here.
    • Milliliters are volume. 236.588 mL of water is about 236.6 g near 4 °C, but a cup of flour, sugar, or honey is a different weight.
    • Imperial cup. An old UK imperial cup is about 284.131 mL (10 imperial fl oz) — not this page.

    FAQ

    How many milliliters are in a cup?

    1 US customary cup = 236.588 mL (exactly 8 US fluid ounces). That is 16 tablespoons or 48 teaspoons.

    Is a cup 240 mL or 250 mL?

    It depends which cup. US customary cup = 236.588 mL. US legal (nutrition label) cup = 240 mL. Metric cup (Australia/Canada and many international recipes) = 250 mL. This page answers the US customary cup.

    How many milliliters are in half a cup?

    118.294 mL (4 US fl oz).
