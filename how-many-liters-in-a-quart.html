    How many liters in a quart?

    1 US liquid quart = 0.946353 L. Same quart: 946.353 mL, 32 fluid ounces, 4 cups, 2 pints, 64 tablespoons, 192 teaspoons. That is exactly 3.785411784 / 4 L (the US liquid gallon of 231 in³), shown to the same rounding as 946.353 mL. This page is the US liquid quart — not the imperial (UK) quart of about 1.136523 L, and not the US dry quart.

    Gotchas

    • US liquid quart vs imperial quart. US liquid quart = 32 US fl oz = 0.946353 L. Imperial (UK) quart = 40 imperial fl oz ≈ 1.136523 L. This page is US liquid only.
    • Liters are volume. 0.946353 L of water is about 946 g near 4 °C, but a quart of flour or honey is a different weight.
    • Liquid quart vs dry quart. A US dry quart is a different measure (~1.101 L) used for some produce. This page is the US liquid quart (32 fl oz / 4 cups).

    FAQ

    How many liters are in a quart?

    1 US liquid quart = 0.946353 L (exactly 3.785411784 / 4 L). That is 946.353 mL, 32 US fluid ounces, 4 US cups, 2 US pints, 64 tablespoons, or 192 teaspoons.

    Is a US quart the same as an imperial quart?

    No. A US liquid quart is 32 US fl oz (0.946353 L). An imperial (UK) quart is 40 imperial fl oz (about 1.136523 L). This page is the US liquid quart.

    Is a quart 1 liter?

    Some charts round a US quart to 1 L (1000 mL). This page is the US liquid quart: 0.946353 L (946.353 mL). Liters are volume, not weight.
