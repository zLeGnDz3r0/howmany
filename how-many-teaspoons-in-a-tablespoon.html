    How many teaspoons in a tablespoon?

    1 US tablespoon = 3 teaspoons. That tablespoon is also 0.5 fluid ounces and about 14.787 mL. These are volume spoons, not grams.

    Gotchas

    • US tsp vs 5 mL metric teaspoon. A US teaspoon is 4.929 mL. Many medicine cups and metric spoons use 5 mL. Fine for a pinch of spice; sloppy for concentrated extracts if you stack many spoons.
    • Australian tablespoon is 20 mL (4 metric teaspoons). A US tablespoon is 3 US teaspoons ≈ 14.787 mL.
    • Fluid ounces ≠ weight ounces. ½ fl oz of vanilla is not ½ ounce on a scale of another ingredient. Spoons measure volume.
    • US cup vs 250 mL metric cup. 48 US teaspoons fill a US cup. A 250 mL cup is about 50.7 US teaspoons.
