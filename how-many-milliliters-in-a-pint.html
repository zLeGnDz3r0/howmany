    How many milliliters in a pint?

    1 US liquid pint = 473.176 mL. Same pint: 16 fluid ounces, 2 cups, 32 tablespoons, 96 teaspoons. That is ½ US liquid quart or ⅛ US liquid gallon. This page is the US liquid pint — not the imperial (UK) pint of about 568.261 mL, and not a 500 mL round.

    Gotchas

    • US pint vs imperial pint. US liquid pint = 16 US fl oz = 473.176 mL. Imperial (UK) pint = 20 imperial fl oz ≈ 568.261 mL. This page is US liquid.
    • Milliliters are volume. 473.176 mL of water is about 473 g near 4 °C, but a pint of flour, sugar, or honey is a different weight.
    • 500 mL round. Some recipes treat a “pint” as 500 mL. That is a convenience round. This page is the US liquid pint: 473.176 mL.

    FAQ

    How many milliliters are in a pint?

    1 US liquid pint = 473.176 mL. That is 16 US fluid ounces, 2 US cups, 32 tablespoons, or 96 teaspoons. Half a US liquid quart; ⅛ of a US liquid gallon.

    Is a US pint the same as an imperial pint?

    No. A US liquid pint is 16 US fl oz (473.176 mL). An imperial (UK) pint is 20 imperial fl oz (about 568.261 mL).

    Is a pint 500 milliliters?

    Some recipes round a “pint” to 500 mL. This page is the US liquid pint: 473.176 mL. Milliliters are volume, not weight.
