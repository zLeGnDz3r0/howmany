    How many milliliters in a quart?

    1 US liquid quart = 946.353 mL. Same quart: 32 fluid ounces, 4 cups, 2 pints, 64 tablespoons, 192 teaspoons. That is ¼ of a US liquid gallon. This page is the US liquid quart — not the imperial (UK) quart of about 1136.52 mL.

    Gotchas

    • US quart vs imperial quart. US liquid quart = 32 US fl oz = 946.353 mL. Imperial (UK) quart = 40 imperial fl oz ≈ 1136.52 mL. This page is US liquid.
    • Milliliters are volume. 946.353 mL of water is about 946 g near 4 °C, but a quart of flour, sugar, or honey is a different weight.
    • Liquid quart vs dry quart. A US dry quart is a different unit (~1.101 L) used for some produce. Kitchen “quart” for milk, stock, and recipes almost always means the liquid quart (32 fl oz / 4 cups).
    • Fluid ounces ≠ weight ounces. 32 fl oz is the volume of a US quart. That is not 32 ounces on a scale unless the ingredient’s density makes it so.

    FAQ

    How many milliliters are in a quart?

    1 US liquid quart = 946.353 mL. That is 32 US fluid ounces, 4 US cups, 2 US pints, 64 tablespoons, or 192 teaspoons. ¼ of a US liquid gallon.

    Is a US quart the same as an imperial quart?

    No. A US liquid quart is 32 US fl oz (946.353 mL). An imperial (UK) quart is 40 imperial fl oz (about 1136.52 mL).

    Is a quart 1 liter?

    Some charts round a US quart to 1 L (1000 mL). This page is the US liquid quart: 946.353 mL. Milliliters are volume, not weight.
