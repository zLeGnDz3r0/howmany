    How many feet in a mile?

    1 US statute mile = 5,280 feet (NIST Handbook 44: 8 furlongs = 1 mile = 5,280 feet). Same mile: 1,760 yards. Related walking question — how many steps in a mile — depends on stride. This page does not.

    Fractions work: 1/2, 1/4. Result is statute feet.

    Gotchas

    • Nautical mile is not 5,280 ft. International nautical mile = 1,852 m (NIST), about 6,076 feet.
    • International foot vs old survey foot. This page uses 1 ft = 0.3048 m exactly, so 1 mile = 1,609.344 m. NIST still tabulates a US survey-foot mile a few millimetres longer. Land records may note which foot they used.
    • Steps are not feet. 5,280 is the length of the mile. How many steps fill it depends on you — steps in a mile.

    Sources

    NIST Handbook 44 (2023) Appendix C: 12 inches = 1 foot; 3 feet = 1 yard; 8 furlongs = 1 US statute mile = 5,280 feet; international nautical mile = 1,852 m. NIST US Survey Foot conversion factors: international-foot mile = 1,609.344 m; international-foot acre listed on the acre page.

    FAQ

    How many feet are in a mile?

    5,280 feet in 1 US statute mile (NIST Handbook 44).

    How many meters is a mile?

    1,609.344 metres using the international foot (1 ft = 0.3048 m exactly).

    Is a nautical mile 5,280 feet?

    No. The international nautical mile is 1,852 metres.

    How many steps in a mile?

    That depends on step length. See the steps-in-a-mile converter.
