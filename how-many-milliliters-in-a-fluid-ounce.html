    How many milliliters in a fluid ounce?

    1 US fluid ounce = 29.574 mL. Same measure: 2 tablespoons, 6 teaspoons, ⅛ cup. That is the US liquid fluid ounce used with the site’s cup (236.588 mL) and spoon pages. Fluid ounces are volume — not weight ounces.

    Gotchas

    • Fluid vs weight. US fluid ounce ≈ 29.574 mL of volume. A weight ounce is about 28.35 g. Do not mix them in a recipe.
    • US vs imperial. This page is the US customary fluid ounce. An imperial (UK) fluid ounce is about 28.413 mL.
    • Label rounding. Some bottles print 30 mL per fl oz. That is a convenience round; the US liquid definition used here is 29.574 mL.

    FAQ

    How many milliliters are in a fluid ounce?

    1 US fluid ounce = 29.574 mL. That is 2 US tablespoons or 6 US teaspoons.

    Is a fluid ounce the same as an ounce?

    No. A US fluid ounce is volume (about 29.574 mL). An ounce of weight is about 28.35 g. They are different units.

    How many milliliters are in 8 fluid ounces?

    236.588 mL — that is 1 US customary cup.
