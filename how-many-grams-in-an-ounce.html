    How many grams in an ounce?

    1 avoirdupois ounce = 28.3495 grams. That is the everyday US kitchen weight ounce (1/16 of a pound). It is not a fluid ounce. Fluid ounces measure volume; ounces here measure mass.

    Gotchas

    • Ounces (weight) ≠ fluid ounces (volume). “How many ounces in a cup?” on this site means 8 fl oz. That cup is 236.588 mL of space. Grams depend on what you put in the cup.
    • Do not convert fl oz to grams with 28.3495 unless you are weighing an ingredient that actually has a mass of one ounce. Water is close (1 mL ≈ 1 g, so 8 fl oz of water ≈ 237 g, not 8 × 28.35). Oil, honey, and flour are not water.
    • Cups to grams need an ingredient. For all-purpose flour, King Arthur lists 1 cup AP flour = 120 g. That is their published cup weight for that flour, not a universal “cup of anything” figure, and not an ounce conversion.
    • Troy ounces are not kitchen ounces. Precious metals use the troy ounce (31.1035 g). Food and postal weight in the US use avoirdupois (28.3495 g).

    FAQ

    How many grams are in an ounce?

    28.3495 g in 1 avoirdupois ounce. 16 ounces = 1 pound = 453.592 g.

    Is this the same as a fluid ounce?

    No. Fluid ounces are volume (see ounces in a cup). This page is weight only.

    How many grams in a cup of flour?

    Not an ounce question. King Arthur lists 1 cup of all-purpose flour as 120 g. See ounces in a cup of flour.
