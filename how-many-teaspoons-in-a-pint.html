    How many teaspoons in a pint?

    1 US liquid pint = 96 US teaspoons. Same pint: 2 cups, 32 tablespoons, 16 fluid ounces, 473.176 mL. Half a pint is 48 tsp. Fluid ounces here are volume, not weight ounces.

    Gotchas

    • US pint vs imperial pint. US liquid pint = 16 US fl oz ≈ 473.176 mL = 96 US tsp. Imperial (UK) pint = 20 imperial fl oz ≈ 568.261 mL. This page is US liquid.
    • Teaspoon size. US customary teaspoon ≈ 4.929 mL. Many metric recipes use a 5 mL teaspoon — close, but not identical if you scale a big batch.
    • Fluid ounces ≠ weight ounces. 96 tsp is 16 fl oz of volume. A pint of water and a pint of honey do not weigh the same.

    FAQ

    How many teaspoons are in a pint?

    There are 96 US teaspoons in 1 US liquid pint (473.176 mL). That is 2 cups, 32 tablespoons, or 16 fluid ounces.

    How many teaspoons are in half a pint?

    48 teaspoons. A quarter pint is 24 teaspoons.

    Is a US pint the same as an imperial pint?

    No. A US liquid pint is 16 US fl oz (473.176 mL), so 96 US teaspoons. An imperial (UK) pint is 20 imperial fl oz (about 568 mL) — a different size.
