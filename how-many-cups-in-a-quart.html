    How many cups in a quart?

    1 US liquid quart = 4 US cups. Same quart: 2 pints, 32 fluid ounces, 946.353 mL. Fluid ounces here are volume, not weight ounces.

    Gotchas

    • US liquid quart vs imperial quart. US quart = 32 US fl oz ≈ 0.946 L. Imperial quart = 1.136 L (40 imperial fl oz). This page is US.
    • US cup vs 250 mL metric cup. Four US cups = 946.353 mL. Four metric cups = 1,000 mL, which is a bit more than a US quart.
    • Fluid ounces ≠ weight ounces. 32 fl oz is the volume of a quart. “32 ounces of stock” on a carton is usually fluid ounces; “32 ounces of cheese” is weight.
