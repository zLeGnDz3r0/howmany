    How many cups in a pint?

    1 US liquid pint = 2 US cups. Same pint: 16 fluid ounces, 32 tablespoons, 96 teaspoons, 473.176 mL. Half a pint is 1 cup (8 fl oz). Fluid ounces here are volume, not weight ounces.

    Gotchas

    • US pint vs imperial pint. US liquid pint = 16 US fl oz ≈ 473.176 mL. Imperial (UK) pint = 20 imperial fl oz ≈ 568.261 mL. This page is US liquid.
    • Liquid pint vs dry pint. A US dry pint is a different unit (~550.610 mL) used for some produce. Kitchen “pint” for milk, stock, and recipes almost always means the liquid pint (2 cups).
    • Fluid ounces ≠ weight ounces. 16 fl oz is the volume of a US pint. That is not 16 ounces on a scale unless the ingredient’s density makes it so.

    FAQ

    How many cups are in a pint?

    2 US cups in 1 US liquid pint.

    How many fluid ounces are in a pint?

    16 US fluid ounces (473.176 mL). Each cup is 8 fl oz, and 2 × 8 = 16.

    Is a US pint the same as an imperial pint?

    No. US liquid pint = 16 US fl oz (473.176 mL). Imperial pint ≈ 568.261 mL (20 imperial fl oz).
