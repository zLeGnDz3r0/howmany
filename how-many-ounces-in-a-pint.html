    How many ounces in a pint?

    1 US liquid pint = 16 US fluid ounces. Same pint: 2 cups, 32 tablespoons, 96 teaspoons, 473.176 mL. Half a pint is 8 fl oz (1 cup). Fluid ounces here are volume, not weight ounces.

    Gotchas

    • US pint vs imperial pint. US liquid pint = 16 US fl oz ≈ 473.176 mL. Imperial (UK) pint = 20 imperial fl oz ≈ 568.261 mL. This page is US liquid.
    • Liquid pint vs dry pint. A US dry pint is a different unit (~550.610 mL) used for some produce. Kitchen “pint” for milk, stock, and recipes almost always means the liquid pint (16 fl oz / 2 cups).
    • Fluid ounces ≠ weight ounces. 16 fl oz is the volume of a US pint. That is not 16 ounces on a scale unless the ingredient’s density makes it so.

    FAQ

    How many ounces are in a pint?

    There are 16 US fluid ounces in 1 US liquid pint (473.176 mL). That is volume, not 16 ounces of weight.

    Is a US pint the same as an imperial pint?

    No. A US liquid pint is 16 US fl oz (473.176 mL). An imperial (UK) pint is 20 imperial fl oz (about 568.261 mL).

    Are fluid ounces the same as weight ounces?

    No. 16 fl oz is the volume of a US pint. That is not 16 ounces on a scale unless the ingredient’s density makes it so.
