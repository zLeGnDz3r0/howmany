    How many ounces in a liter?

    1 liter = 33.814 US fl oz. Same liter: about 4.22675 US cups, 2.11338 US pints, 1.05669 US quarts, 1000 mL, 67.628 US tablespoons, 202.884 US teaspoons. That is 128 / 3.785411784, because 1 US liquid gallon is exactly 3.785411784 L (231 in³) and holds 128 US fl oz. 1 US fl oz ≈ 29.5735 mL. These are fluid ounces (volume), not avoirdupois weight ounces.

    Gotchas

    • Fluid ounces ≠ weight ounces. 33.814 fl oz is a volume. An avoirdupois ounce is weight (28.3495 g). A liter of water weighs about 1 kg near 4 °C; a liter of flour, oil, or honey is a different weight. Do not treat “ounces in a liter” as a scale reading.
    • US fl oz vs imperial fl oz. 1 liter = 33.814 US fl oz, and about 35.195 imperial fl oz. The US liquid gallon is exactly 3.785411784 L and 128 US fl oz. An imperial fluid ounce is a different volume. Do not use this factor for UK imperial measures.
    • Liters are volume. 1 L = 1000 mL. A liter measures space, not mass. 1 L of water weighs about 1 kg near 4 °C, but other liquids and dry goods do not.
