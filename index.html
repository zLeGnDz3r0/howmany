    Gotchas

    • US cup vs metric cup vs legal cup. Default here is the US customary cup: 8 fl oz = 236.588 mL. A metric cup is 250 mL. The US legal cup on nutrition labels is 240 mL. Use the cup-standard control — don’t mix them.
    • Fluid ounces are not weight ounces. “8 ounces in a cup” means 8 fluid ounces of volume. An ounce of flour is weight (28.3495 g). 1 US cup of all-purpose flour is 120 g (King Arthur), about 4¼ oz by weight — not 8 oz. See fluid ounces vs ounces.
    • US vs imperial. A US fluid ounce is 29.5735 mL. An imperial (UK) fluid ounce is 28.4130625 mL. A US gallon is 128 US fl oz; an imperial gallon is 160 imperial fl oz.

    US customary liquid measures. Fluid ounces are volume, not weight. Switch cup standard on the converter if you need a 240 mL, 250 mL, or imperial cup.
