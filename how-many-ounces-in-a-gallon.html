    How many ounces in a gallon?

    1 US gallon = 128 US fluid ounces. Same gallon: 16 cups, 8 pints, 4 quarts, 3.785 L. These ounces are volume (fl oz), not ounces of weight.

    Gotchas

    • Fluid ounces ≠ weight ounces. 128 fl oz is a volume. A gallon of water is about 8.34 pounds; a gallon of honey is heavier. Do not treat “ounces in a gallon” as a scale reading unless you know the density.
    • US gallon vs imperial gallon. US gal = 3.785411784 L = 128 US fl oz. Imperial gal = 4.54609 L. Milk jugs in the US are US gallons.
    • US cup vs 250 mL metric cup. 16 US cups fill a US gallon. Sixteen 250 mL metric cups are 4.0 L, which is more than a US gallon.
