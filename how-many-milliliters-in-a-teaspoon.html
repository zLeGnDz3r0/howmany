    How many milliliters in a teaspoon?

    1 US teaspoon = 4.929 mL. Same spoon: ⅙ fluid ounce, ⅓ tablespoon. That is the exact US liquid teaspoon (1/48 of a US cup). Metric recipes often round to 5 mL.

    Gotchas

    • US vs 5 mL. US customary teaspoon = 4.929 mL (⅙ US fl oz). Many metric cookbooks treat 1 tsp as 5 mL. This page is the US spoon.
    • Milliliters are volume. 4.929 mL of water is about 4.9 g near 4 °C, but a teaspoon of salt, sugar, or oil is a different weight.
    • Level vs heaping. These figures are level teaspoons of liquid measure — not heaping dry scoops.

    FAQ

    How many milliliters are in a teaspoon?

    1 US teaspoon = 4.929 mL (exactly ⅙ US fluid ounce). That is ⅓ of a US tablespoon.

    Is a teaspoon 5 mL or 4.929 mL?

    US customary teaspoon = 4.929 mL (⅙ US fl oz). Many metric recipes round a teaspoon to 5 mL. This page answers the US customary teaspoon.

    How many milliliters are in a tablespoon?

    1 US tablespoon = 14.787 mL (exactly ½ US fluid ounce). That is 3 teaspoons.
