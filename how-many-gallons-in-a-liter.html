    How many gallons in a liter?

    1 liter = 0.264172 US gallons. Same liter: about 1.05669 US quarts, 2.11338 US pints, 4.22675 US cups, 33.814 US fl oz, 1000 mL. That is 1 / 3.785411784 ≈ 0.264172052, because 1 US liquid gallon is exactly 3.785411784 L (231 in³, NIST Handbook 44 Appendix C). This is the US liquid gallon, not the imperial gallon (~0.219969 gal per liter). Gallons are volume, not weight.

    Gotchas

    • US liquid gallon ≠ imperial gallon. 1 liter = 0.264172 US liquid gallons. An imperial (UK) gallon is 4.54609 L, so 1 liter is about 0.219969 imperial gallons. Do not use this factor for UK imperial measures.
    • Gallons are volume, not weight. A gallon measures space. 1 L of water weighs about 1 kg near 4 °C; a liter of flour, oil, or honey is a different weight. Do not treat gallons in a liter as a scale reading.
    • Fluid ounces ≠ weight ounces. The same liter is about 33.814 US fl oz. A US fluid ounce is volume (about 29.5735 mL). An avoirdupois ounce is weight (28.3495 g). Do not read 33.814 as ounces on a scale.
