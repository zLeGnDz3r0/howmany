    How many teaspoons in a cup?

    1 US cup = 48 teaspoons. That is also 16 tablespoons and 8 fluid ounces (236.588 mL). Half a cup is 24 tsp; a quarter cup is 12 tsp.

    Gotchas

    • US teaspoon vs metric teaspoon. A US tsp is 1⁄6 US fl oz ≈ 4.929 mL. Many metric recipes round a teaspoon to 5 mL. Close for cooking, not identical for precise baking.
    • US cup vs 250 mL metric cup. 48 US tsp fill a US cup (236.588 mL). A 250 mL metric cup is about 50.7 US tsp.
    • Fluid ounces ≠ weight ounces. 48 teaspoons of volume is 8 fl oz. That is not 8 ounces on a scale unless the ingredient’s density makes it so.
