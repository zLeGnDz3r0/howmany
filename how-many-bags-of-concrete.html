    Gotchas

    • Yield is mixed volume, not bag size on the shelf. An 80 lb bag is 80 pounds of dry mix. After water it occupies about 0.60 cubic feet (QUIKRETE TDS).
    • Waste is your number. The data sheet does not say “add 10%.” Leave it at 0, or set extra for spillage and uneven subgrade. It is labeled as an assumption.
    • 25 kg is not a universal EU bag. The 11.7 L figure is a sourced 25 kg (55 lb) packaged mix yield. Other European bags can differ; we do not invent them.
    • This is not a structural design. Footings, reinforcement, and mix strength belong to local code and a contractor. QUIKRETE mix is for pours 2 in (50 mm) thick or more.

    FAQ

    How many 80 lb bags of concrete do I need?

    Divide the pour volume in cubic feet by 0.60. QUIKRETE lists an 80 lb bag at about 0.60 ft³ (17 L). Round up to whole bags.

    How much does a 60 lb or 40 lb bag yield?

    QUIKRETE Concrete Mix TDS: 60 lb ≈ 0.45 ft³ (12.7 L); 40 lb ≈ 0.30 ft³ (8.5 L).

    Do you include a waste factor?

    Only if you type one. Waste is an optional assumption you set, not a QUIKRETE number.

    What about 25 kg bags?

    A sourced 25 kg (55 lb) packaged mix bag is about 11.7 L (0.41 cu ft). Not a generic EU yield.
