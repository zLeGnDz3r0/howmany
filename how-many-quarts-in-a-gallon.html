    How many quarts in a gallon?

    1 US gallon = 4 US quarts. Same gallon: 8 pints, 16 cups, 128 fluid ounces, 3.785 L. Fluid ounces are volume, not weight ounces.

    Gotchas

    • US vs imperial. 4 US quarts = 1 US gallon = 3.785411784 L. An imperial gallon is 4 imperial quarts = 4.54609 L. A quart of milk in the US is the smaller unit.
    • US cup vs 250 mL metric cup. A US quart is 4 US cups (946.353 mL), not 4 × 250 mL.
    • Fluid ounces ≠ weight ounces. 128 fl oz in a gallon is volume. Stock sold as 32 fl oz (1 quart) is volume; deli meat sold by the ounce is weight.
