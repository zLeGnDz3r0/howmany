    How many ounces in a cup?

    1 US cup = 8 US fluid ounces. Same cup: 16 tablespoons, 48 teaspoons, 236.588 mL. These ounces are volume (fl oz), not ounces of weight.

    Gotchas

    • Fluid ounces ≠ ounces (weight). A cup of water is about 8 fl oz and also about 8.3 oz by weight (because 1 mL of water ≈ 1 g). A cup of flour is still 8 fl oz of volume, but far less than 8 ounces on a scale.
    • US cup vs 250 mL metric cup. US customary cup = 236.588 mL. Metric cup = 250 mL (about 8.45 US fl oz). Nutrition-label “cup” in the US is 240 mL.
    • US vs imperial fluid ounce. US fl oz = 29.5735 mL. Imperial fl oz = 28.413 mL. British recipes that say “8 ounces” of liquid are not this cup.
