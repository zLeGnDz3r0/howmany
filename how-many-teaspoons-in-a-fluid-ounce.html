    How many teaspoons in a fluid ounce?

    1 US fluid ounce = 6 teaspoons. Same measure: 2 tablespoons and about 29.574 mL. 1 US tablespoon = 3 tsp = 0.5 fl oz, so 1 US teaspoon is about 4.929 mL. Fluid ounces are volume — not weight ounces. This is the US fluid ounce, not the imperial fluid ounce.

    Gotchas

    • Fluid vs weight. A US fluid ounce is 6 teaspoons of volume (about 29.574 mL). An avoirdupois ounce is weight. Do not mix them in a recipe.
    • US vs imperial. This page is the US customary fluid ounce (2 US tablespoons, 6 US teaspoons). An imperial (UK) fluid ounce is a different volume.
    • US tsp vs 5 mL metric teaspoon. 1 US teaspoon is about 4.929 mL (1/6 of 29.574 mL). Many medicine cups and metric spoons use 5 mL. 1 US tablespoon = 3 tsp = 0.5 fl oz.

    FAQ

    How many teaspoons are in a fluid ounce?

    There are 6 US teaspoons in 1 US fluid ounce. That is 2 US tablespoons. 1 US teaspoon is about 4.929 mL (1/6 of 29.574 mL).

    Is a fluid ounce the same as an ounce?

    No. A US fluid ounce is volume (6 teaspoons, about 29.574 mL). An avoirdupois ounce is weight. They are different units.

    Is a US fluid ounce the same as an imperial fluid ounce?

    No. This page is the US fluid ounce: 2 US tablespoons, or 6 US teaspoons. An imperial fluid ounce is a different volume.
