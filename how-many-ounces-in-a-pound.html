    How many ounces in a pound?

    1 avoirdupois pound = 16 avoirdupois ounces (NIST Handbook 44). These are weight ounces, not fluid ounces. A US cup is 8 fl oz of volume — that is the kitchen trap. See fluid ounces vs ounces.

    Fractions work: 1/2, 1 1/2. Result is avoirdupois ounces.

    Gotchas

    • Fluid ounces are not these ounces. 16 ounces in a pound is mass. 8 ounces in a cup is fluid ounces of volume. Grams in an ounce is the same weight ounce (28.3495 g).
    • Troy is 12, not 16. A troy pound is 12 troy ounces (precious metals). Everyday food and body weight use avoirdupois (16).
    • 1 lb = 453.59237 g exactly in the NIST avoirdupois definition (0.45359237 kg). 16 × 28.349523125 g = 453.59237 g.

    Sources

    NIST Handbook 44 (2023) Appendix C: 1 avoirdupois pound = 16 avoirdupois ounces; 1 lb = 0.45359237 kg; 1 oz = 28.349523125 g. Same international pound used on this site’s grams-in-an-ounce page.
