    How many teaspoons in a quart?

    1 US liquid quart = 192 US teaspoons. Same quart: 4 cups, 2 pints, 64 tablespoons, 32 fluid ounces, 946.353 mL. Half a quart is 96 tsp. Fluid ounces here are volume, not weight ounces.

    Gotchas

    • US quart vs imperial quart. US liquid quart = 32 US fl oz ≈ 946.353 mL = 192 US tsp. Imperial (UK) quart = 40 imperial fl oz ≈ 1.1365 L. This page is US liquid.
    • Teaspoon size. US customary teaspoon ≈ 4.929 mL. Many metric recipes use a 5 mL teaspoon — close, but not identical if you scale a big batch.
    • Fluid ounces ≠ weight ounces. 192 tsp is 32 fl oz of volume. A quart of water and a quart of honey do not weigh the same.

    FAQ

    How many teaspoons are in a quart?

    There are 192 US teaspoons in 1 US liquid quart (946.353 mL). That is 4 cups, 2 pints, 64 tablespoons, or 32 fluid ounces.

    How many teaspoons are in half a quart?

    96 teaspoons. A quarter quart is 48 teaspoons (1 cup).

    Is a US quart the same as an imperial quart?

    No. A US liquid quart is 32 US fl oz (946.353 mL), so 192 US teaspoons. An imperial (UK) quart is 40 imperial fl oz (about 1.137 L) — a different size.
