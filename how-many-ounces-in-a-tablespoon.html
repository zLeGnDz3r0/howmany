    How many ounces in a tablespoon?

    1 US tablespoon = 0.5 US fluid ounces. Same measure: 3 teaspoons and about 14.787 mL. 1 US fluid ounce = 2 US tablespoons, so 1 US tablespoon is exactly half a US fl oz. Fluid ounces are volume — not weight ounces. This is the US tablespoon and US fluid ounce, not imperial.

    Gotchas

    • Fluid vs weight. A US tablespoon is 0.5 US fluid ounces of volume (about 14.787 mL). An avoirdupois ounce is weight. Do not mix them in a recipe.
    • US vs imperial. This page is the US tablespoon and the US fluid ounce: 1 US tbsp = 0.5 US fl oz ≈ 14.787 mL. An imperial fluid ounce is about 28.413 mL, not 29.574 mL. A metric tablespoon is often 15 mL; an Australian tablespoon is 20 mL.
    • Reverse. 1 US fluid ounce = 2 US tablespoons. See tablespoons in a fluid ounce.

    Is a fluid ounce the same as an ounce?

    No. A US fluid ounce is volume. 1 US tablespoon is 0.5 US fl oz (about 14.787 mL). An avoirdupois ounce is weight. They are different units.

    Is a US tablespoon the same as an imperial tablespoon?

    No. This page is the US tablespoon: 0.5 US fluid ounces, 3 US teaspoons, about 14.787 mL. An imperial fluid ounce is about 28.413 mL, and UK or Australian tablespoons are different spoons.
