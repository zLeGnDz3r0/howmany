    How many cups in a liter?

    1 liter = 4.22675 US cups. Same liter: about 33.814 US fl oz, 2.11338 US pints, 1.05669 US quarts, 1000 mL, 67.628 US tablespoons, 202.884 US teaspoons. That is 1 / 0.2365882365, because 1 US cup is exactly 1/16 of a US liquid gallon (3.785411784 L, 231 in³). This page is the US customary cup — not the metric cup (250 mL) and not the US legal cup (240 mL).

    Gotchas

    • US cup vs metric cup vs legal cup. US customary cup = 236.5882365 mL (8 US fl oz), so 1 L = 4.22675 cups. A metric cup is 250 mL, so 1 L is exactly 4 metric cups. A US legal cup on nutrition labels is 240 mL (about 4.16667 cups per liter). This page is the US customary cup.
    • Liters are volume. 1 L of water weighs about 1 kg near 4 °C, but a liter of flour, oil, or honey is a different weight.
    • US customary, not imperial. These cups are US customary liquid cups. An imperial cup is a different volume. Do not use this factor for UK imperial measures.

    FAQ

    How many cups are in a liter?

    1 liter = 4.22675 US customary cups. That is about 33.814 US fl oz, 2.11338 US pints, 1.05669 US quarts, 1000 mL, 67.628 US tablespoons, or 202.884 US teaspoons. 1 US cup = 0.2365882365 L (gallon/16; the US liquid gallon is exactly 3.785411784 L).

    Is a liter 4 cups?

    A metric cup is 250 mL, so 1 L is exactly 4 metric cups. This page is the US customary cup: 4.22675 cups. A US legal cup is 240 mL (about 4.16667 cups per liter). It is not an imperial cup.

    Is a liter a measure of weight?

    No. A liter is volume. 1 L of water weighs about 1 kg near 4 °C. A liter of flour, oil, or honey is a different weight.
