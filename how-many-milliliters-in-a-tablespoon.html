    How many milliliters in a tablespoon?

    1 US tablespoon = 14.787 mL. Same spoon: ½ fluid ounce, 3 teaspoons. That is the exact US liquid tablespoon (1/32 of a US cup). Metric recipes often round to 15 mL; an Australian tablespoon is 20 mL.

    Gotchas

    • US vs 15 mL vs 20 mL. US customary tablespoon = 14.787 mL (½ US fl oz). Many metric cookbooks treat 1 tbsp as 15 mL. An Australian tablespoon is 20 mL (4 teaspoons of 5 mL). This page is the US spoon.
    • Milliliters are volume. 14.787 mL of water is about 14.8 g near 4 °C, but a tablespoon of flour, sugar, or honey is a different weight.
    • Level vs heaping. These figures are level tablespoons of liquid measure — not heaping dry scoops.

    FAQ

    How many milliliters are in a tablespoon?

    1 US tablespoon = 14.787 mL (exactly ½ US fluid ounce). That is 3 teaspoons.

    Is a tablespoon 15 mL or 14.787 mL?

    US customary tablespoon = 14.787 mL (½ US fl oz). Many metric recipes round a tablespoon to 15 mL. An Australian tablespoon is 20 mL. This page answers the US customary tablespoon.

    How many milliliters are in a teaspoon?

    1 US teaspoon = 4.929 mL (⅓ of a US tablespoon).
