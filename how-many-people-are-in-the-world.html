    How many people are in the world?

    About 8.2 billion people at mid-year 2024, per the UN World Population Prospects 2024 (Summary of Results, published 11 July 2024). The same revision’s mid-year 2024 estimate is 8,161,973 thousand. This figure moves with each revision. It is not a live counter.

    Gotchas

    • No live counter here. World-population clocks interpolate births and deaths between estimates. They are not a UN census of “right now.” We show the dated WPP 2024 figures instead.
    • 8.2 billion is a rounding. The UNSD Vital Statistics table citing WPP 2024 lists 8,161,973 thousand for mid-year 2024 (about 8.162 billion). The Summary of Results headlines 8.2 billion for 2024. Same revision, different rounding.
    • Revisions replace earlier numbers. WPP is updated on a multi-year cycle. A 2022 or 2019 figure is a previous revision, not a live update of this page. Peak and 2100 figures are projections (medium variant), not counts.

    FAQ

    How many people are in the world?

    About 8.2 billion at mid-year 2024, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024 (Summary of Results, published 11 July 2024). The same revision’s mid-year 2024 figure is 8,161,973 thousand. This is a dated estimate, not a live count.

    Is this a live world-population clock?

    No. Live tickers interpolate between estimates. This page shows the UN’s published 2024 figures and the date they refer to.

    When did the world reach 8 billion?

    The United Nations marks 15 November 2022 as the 8 billion milestone. WPP 2024 then estimates about 8.2 billion at mid-year 2024.

    Will this number keep growing?

    WPP 2024 projects a peak around 10.3 billion in the mid-2080s (medium variant), then a slight decline toward 10.2 billion by 2100. Those are projections, not counts.
