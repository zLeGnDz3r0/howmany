    How many liters in a cup?

    1 US customary cup = 0.236588 L. Same cup: 236.588 mL, 8 fluid ounces, 16 tablespoons, 48 teaspoons. That is the exact US liquid cup (1/16 of a US gallon of 3.785411784 L). A metric cup is 0.25 L; the US nutrition-label cup is 0.24 L.

    Gotchas

    • US cup vs 0.24 L vs 0.25 L. US customary cup = 0.236588 L (8 US fl oz, 236.588 mL). US legal cup on nutrition labels = 0.24 L (240 mL). Metric cup = 0.25 L (250 mL). NIST home-cooking charts often round the US cup to 240 mL — close, but not the exact customary value used here.
    • Liters are volume. 0.236588 L of water is about 236.6 g near 4 °C, but a cup of flour, sugar, or honey is a different weight.
    • Imperial cup. An old UK imperial cup is about 0.284131 L (10 imperial fl oz) — not this page.

    FAQ

    How many liters are in a cup?

    1 US customary cup = 0.236588 L (exactly 8 US fluid ounces). That is 236.588 mL, 16 tablespoons, or 48 teaspoons.

    Is a cup 0.24 L or 0.25 L?

    It depends which cup. US customary cup = 0.236588 L. US legal (nutrition label) cup = 0.24 L (240 mL). Metric cup = 0.25 L (250 mL). This page answers the US customary cup.

    How many liters are in half a cup?

    0.118294 L (118.294 mL, 4 US fl oz).
