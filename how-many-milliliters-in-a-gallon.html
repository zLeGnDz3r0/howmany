    How many milliliters in a gallon?

    1 US liquid gallon = 3785.412 mL. Same gallon: 128 fluid ounces, 16 cups, 8 pints, 4 quarts, 256 tablespoons, 768 teaspoons. Exact figure is 3785.411784 mL (NIST: 231 cubic inches). This page is the US liquid gallon — not the imperial (UK) gallon of about 4546.09 mL.

    Gotchas

    • US gallon vs imperial gallon. US liquid gallon = 128 US fl oz = 3785.412 mL. Imperial (UK) gallon = 160 imperial fl oz ≈ 4546.09 mL. This page is US liquid.
    • Milliliters are volume. 3785.412 mL of water is about 3785 g near 4 °C, but a gallon of flour, sugar, or honey is a different weight.
    • Liquid gallon vs dry gallon. A US dry gallon is a different unit (268.8025 in³ in NIST tables), not 231 in³. Kitchen “gallon” for milk, stock, and recipes almost always means the liquid gallon (128 fl oz / 16 cups).
    • Fluid ounces ≠ weight ounces. 128 fl oz is the volume of a US gallon. That is not 128 ounces on a scale unless the ingredient’s density makes it so.

    How many milliliters are in a gallon?

    Is a US gallon the same as an imperial gallon?

    Is a gallon 4 liters?

    Some charts round a US gallon to 4 L (4000 mL). This page is the US liquid gallon: 3785.412 mL. Milliliters are volume, not weight.
