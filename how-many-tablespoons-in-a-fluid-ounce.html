    How many tablespoons in a fluid ounce?

    1 US fluid ounce = 2 tablespoons. Same measure: 6 teaspoons and about 29.574 mL. 1 US tablespoon = 0.5 US fluid ounce = 3 teaspoons ≈ 14.787 mL. Fluid ounces are volume — not weight ounces. This is the US fluid ounce, not the imperial fluid ounce.

    Gotchas

    • Fluid vs weight. A US fluid ounce is 2 tablespoons of volume (about 29.574 mL). An avoirdupois ounce is weight. Do not mix them in a recipe.
    • US vs imperial. This page is the US customary fluid ounce (2 US tablespoons, 6 US teaspoons). An imperial (UK) fluid ounce is a different volume.
    • US tbsp vs 15 mL metric tablespoon. 1 US tablespoon is 0.5 fl oz, about 14.787 mL (half of 29.574 mL), and equals 3 teaspoons. Many metric spoons use 15 mL. An Australian tablespoon is 20 mL.
