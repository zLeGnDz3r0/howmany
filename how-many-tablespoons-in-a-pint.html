    How many tablespoons in a pint?

    1 US liquid pint = 32 US tablespoons. Same pint: 2 cups, 16 fluid ounces, 96 teaspoons, 473.176 mL. Half a pint is 16 tbsp. Fluid ounces here are volume, not weight ounces.

    Gotchas

    • US pint vs imperial pint. US liquid pint = 16 US fl oz ≈ 473.176 mL = 32 US tbsp. Imperial (UK) pint = 20 imperial fl oz ≈ 568.261 mL. This page is US liquid.
    • Tablespoon size. US customary tablespoon = ½ US fl oz ≈ 14.787 mL. Australian tablespoon is 20 mL — not used here.
    • Fluid ounces ≠ weight ounces. 32 tbsp is 16 fl oz of volume. A pint of milk and a pint of honey do not weigh the same.

    FAQ

    How many tablespoons are in a pint?

    There are 32 US tablespoons in 1 US liquid pint (473.176 mL). That is 2 cups, 16 fluid ounces, or 96 teaspoons.

    Is a US pint the same as an imperial pint?

    No. A US liquid pint is 16 US fl oz (473.176 mL), so 32 US tablespoons. An imperial (UK) pint is 20 imperial fl oz (568.261 mL) — a different size.

    How many tablespoons are in a cup?

    16 US tablespoons. A US pint is 2 cups, so 32 tablespoons.
