    How many tablespoons in a gallon?

    1 US liquid gallon = 256 US tablespoons. Same gallon: 16 cups, 8 pints, 4 quarts, 128 fluid ounces, 768 teaspoons, 3,785.412 mL. Half a gallon is 128 tbsp. Fluid ounces here are volume, not weight ounces.

    Gotchas

    • US gallon vs imperial gallon. US liquid gallon = 128 US fl oz ≈ 3,785.412 mL = 256 US tbsp. Imperial (UK) gallon = 160 imperial fl oz ≈ 4.54609 L. This page is US liquid.
    • Tablespoon size. US customary tablespoon = ½ US fl oz ≈ 14.787 mL. Australian tablespoon is 20 mL — not used here.
    • Fluid ounces ≠ weight ounces. 256 tbsp is 128 fl oz of volume. A gallon of water and a gallon of honey do not weigh the same.

    FAQ

    How many tablespoons are in a gallon?

    There are 256 US tablespoons in 1 US liquid gallon (3,785.412 mL). That is 16 cups, 8 pints, 4 quarts, 128 fluid ounces, or 768 teaspoons.

    Is a US gallon the same as an imperial gallon?

    No. A US liquid gallon is 128 US fl oz (3,785.412 mL), so 256 US tablespoons. An imperial (UK) gallon is 160 imperial fl oz (about 4.546 L) — a different size.

    How many tablespoons are in a quart?

    64 US tablespoons. A US gallon is 4 quarts, so 256 tablespoons.
